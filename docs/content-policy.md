# Content policy

Source cases are attributed to the original author and collecting repository, with pinned source links. The gallery references upstream image URLs without downloading or rehosting them. The prompt field preserves the text published by the source; the Markdown presentation flattens line breaks for horizontal scrolling and copying. Third-party prompts and images retain their respective owners’ rights. Inclusion, attribution and public accessibility do not establish permission for redistribution or commercial use. Unverified rights remain marked `unknown`; they are not licensed under this project's MIT license. Rights holders can request correction or removal via issues. Do not claim that examples have been independently generated or tested. No fabricated before/after examples.

Content governance metadata is generated on each build. Cases without a real prompt or image URL are withheld from the gallery, while their original records remain indexed. Duplicate groups are review candidates; metadata completeness does not imply visual or rights verification. See [governance report](governance-report.md).

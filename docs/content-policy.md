# Content policy

Source cases are attributed to the original author and collecting repository, with pinned source links. The visual gallery references upstream image URLs without downloading or rehosting them. Image rights remain with their owners; an external URL does not imply permission. Short prompt excerpts are shown for discovery; complete prompts remain at the source unless explicit redistribution permission is obtained. Image roles are unverified except where the source identifies them. Source corrections, removal requests and rights claims can be submitted via issues. No fabricated before/after examples.

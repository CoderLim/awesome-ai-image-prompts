```text

Back-left subject: standing behind the center subject, making the same heart shape with both hands, with a subtle friendly smile. Transform into a young man with natural dark wavy hair while keeping the exact clothing, pose and position. Back-right subject: standing behind the center subject, giving the same peace/V sign with one hand and smiling toward the camera. Transform into a young man with naturally styled dark wavy hair, preserving the exact pose and outfit. Front-left subject: crouching beside the center subject with one hand supporting his cheek, maintaining the same relaxed smiling expression, outfit, body position and framing. Transform into a young man with natural dark wavy hair. Front-right subject: crouching with both hands supporting his cheeks and making the same playful expression while looking slightly upward. Transform into a young man with natural dark wavy hair, preserving the exact pose, clothing and position. All five men should look like realistic young adult male versions, with masculine but natural facial features, realistic skin texture, subtle imperfections, believable anatomy, and consistent facial identity across the transformation. Their smiles and facial expressions must remain as close as possible to the original reference. Keep the olive/khaki coordinated outfits, white sneakers, black watches, black stool, wooden floor, patterned rug, wooden box, books, black backpack, plants, studio lights, chalkboards and all background elements exactly as shown. Preserve the background text and props where possible, including “SAME GUY DIFFERENT MOODS”, “Good Ideas Different You”, “DISCIPLINE / CREATIVITY / BETTER YOU”, and “Still Me”. Warm cinematic studio lighting, realistic shadows, natural skin tones, detailed hair strands, accurate hands and fingers, realistic clothing folds, professional editorial photography, photorealistic, high detail, sharp focus, natural depth of field, consistent perspective. Do not change the composition or reposition anyone. Do not add or remove people. Do not change the camera angle. The final image should look like the exact same original photograph, but with all five female subjects naturally transformed into male versions.

**Prompt**

```text
raw photo captured with Hasselblad X1D II 50C, low key lighting, high micro-contrast, ISO 100, with a 50mm prime lens. Cinematic hyper-realistic night photograph of a beautiful young woman with the exact same face, facial features, blue eyes, nose, lips, expression and ultra-long blonde hair with horn-shaped buns and bangs as the woman in the reference image. She has a voluptuous hourglass figure with full bust, slim waist, wide hips and athletic thick legs. She is leaning casually against a red 2025 Chevrolet Corvette Stingray with white racing stripes, full aggressive body kit and Japanese decals on the hood. She wears a sporty black leather jacket, a very short black leather mini skirt and knee-high leather boots. She carries a black backpack on her back and has both hands in her jacket pockets. She is looking up toward the camera with a top-down angle, exuding a mysterious and seductive presence. Surface micro-textures include individual pores showing sebaceous secretions, fine sweat on her skin, realistic skin imperfections, subtle film grain and natural texture on her skin, hair and leather clothing. The glossy red car paint, leather jacket and mini skirt have rich, authentic reflections and material details. Lighting follows dual-source Chiaroscuro: dramatic neon blue and metallic red street lighting from the city creates strong contrast and vibrant reflections on the car and her skin, while soft cool ambient light from the side illuminates her face and figure. Glistening highlights define her voluptuous curves with luminous, natural skin tones. Atmospheric physics incorporate Mie scattering due to sub-micron particulate load in the night air, creating gentle volumetric haze and soft neon bokeh. The background is a wet nighttime roadside with dark asphalt, white lane markings, tiled sidewalk and glowing futuristic city lights. Rich, high-contrast cinematic palette with dominant neon blue, metallic red and warm streetlight tones provides a dynamic, mysterious and highly photorealistic Japanese street racing atmosphere.
```

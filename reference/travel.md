## Three-Scene Watercolor Travel Journal

<a href="https://pub-62cf7640cd0f4066b60933bd2e9b85ef.r2.dev/github-repo-media/awesome-gpt-image-2-5-prompts/assets/media/2103772070840860672.jpg"><img src="https://pub-62cf7640cd0f4066b60933bd2e9b85ef.r2.dev/github-repo-media/awesome-gpt-image-2-5-prompts/assets/media/2103772070840860672.jpg" width="480" alt="Source example"></a>

**Prompt**

```text
Create a refined luxury editorial travel-journal composition based on all three uploaded reference images, combining three distinct travel moments into one cohesive artwork: a tropical Maldives beach seen from an aerial perspective, a crystal-clear rocky Mediterranean-style swimming cove with four swimmers, and an elegant London city street featuring a classic red double-decker bus surrounded by historic architecture and pedestrians. Upper section: create a realistic cinematic travel-photography composition that seamlessly brings these three destinations together as a sophisticated visual travel story. Preserve the recognizable details from each reference: the Maldives scene with brilliant turquoise shallow water, white sandy beach, lush coconut palms and several swimmers including a person relaxing on a yellow/orange float; the rocky blue-water cove with four swimmers positioned naturally in the water, surrounded by rugged sunlit rocks and visible underwater textures; and the London street with the iconic red double-decker bus, historic cream-stone buildings, shopfronts, pedestrians, street lamps and authentic urban atmosphere. Use natural daylight, realistic water reflections, detailed textures, subtle film grain, authentic candid activity, elegant muted color grading and high-end travel magazine photography. Lower section: transform the same combined travel scenes into delicate handmade watercolor illustrations while preserving the recognizable composition and major visual elements from all three references. Keep the Maldives beach, palm trees, turquoise water and swimmers; the rocky swimming cove, four swimmers and surrounding rocks; and the London double-decker bus, historic buildings, pedestrians and street details clearly recognizable. Use loose translucent watercolor washes, delicate ink details, soft edges, imperfect hand-painted brushwork, subtle pigment bleeding, visible handmade paper texture, airy negative space and sophisticated pastel tones. The watercolor must look like a faithful artistic recreation of the photographic scenes, not a completely different interpretation. Arrange the three destinations harmoniously as one elegant travel story with smooth visual transitions, balanced proportions and a premium editorial layout. Surround the watercolor artwork with generous warm ivory/off-white textured paper. Add a small elegant handwritten caption near the bottom reading “summer days — destinations” with a thin minimalist horizontal line underneath. Overall aesthetic: luxury travel journal, fine-art watercolor postcard, contemporary editorial design, sophisticated wanderlust, nostalgic summer travel, tactile handmade paper, serene and premium art direction, realistic photography transitioning into delicate watercolor illustration, clean composition, natural proportions, no clutter, no unnecessary borders. Portrait orientation, high-end travel magazine quality, cinematic realism, detailed watercolor rendering, cohesive storytelling, balanced vertical composition, preserve the exact major elements and relative placement of the three reference scenes, no duplicated people
```

## Clear Pool Travel Journal

<a href="https://pub-62cf7640cd0f4066b60933bd2e9b85ef.r2.dev/github-repo-media/awesome-gpt-image-2-5-prompts/assets/media/2103514824810360832.jpg"><img src="https://pub-62cf7640cd0f4066b60933bd2e9b85ef.r2.dev/github-repo-media/awesome-gpt-image-2-5-prompts/assets/media/2103514824810360832.jpg" width="480" alt="Source example"></a>

**Prompt**

```text
Create a refined editorial travel-art composition based on the uploaded reference images. Combine the two references into one cohesive summer travel-journal artwork featuring a crystal-clear turquoise natural swimming lagoon viewed from above, surrounded by rugged pale limestone rocks and shallow transparent water. Preserve the exact arrangement and natural positions of the five swimmers: one woman relaxing in a circular float near the upper-left, one swimmer moving through the center, one swimmer in a pink swimsuit below center, one person swimming toward the lower-right, and one person standing/wading near the lower-left. Keep their relative positions, poses, scale, swimwear, water ripples, rocks and overall aerial perspective recognizable. Upper section: realistic cinematic overhead travel photography of the entire scene, brilliant transparent turquoise water, visible stones beneath the surface, natural sunlight creating detailed water reflections and caustic patterns, realistic swimmers and natural candid summer activity, rugged rocky shoreline, subtle film grain, authentic textures, sophisticated high-end travel magazine photography, soft natural color grading, bright but elegant summer atmosphere. Lower section: transform the exact same swimming scene into a delicate handmade watercolor illustration while preserving the same aerial composition, five people, their positions and poses, rocky formations, water movement and major visual details. Use loose translucent watercolor washes, delicate ink details, soft blue and aqua pigments, subtle green and beige tones, visible handmade paper texture, imperfect brushwork, gentle pigment bleeding, soft edges, natural watercolor splashes and airy negative space. The watercolor should clearly feel like a hand-painted artistic recreation of the photograph, not a different scene. Surround the watercolor artwork with generous warm ivory/off-white textured paper. Keep the transition between the realistic photograph and watercolor clean and elegant. Add a small minimalist handwritten caption near the bottom reading “summer days”, followed by a thin delicate horizontal line underneath. Overall aesthetic: luxury summer travel journal, fine-art watercolor postcard, contemporary editorial design, nostalgic vacation atmosphere, sophisticated yet relaxed, tactile handmade paper, premium art direction, serene natural beauty, clean composition, no clutter, no unnecessary borders, no extra people, no duplicated swimmers, no distorted bodies. Portrait orientation, balanced vertical layout, realistic-to-watercolor transformation, highly detailed photography, delicate artistic watercolor rendering, natural proportions, premium travel magazine quality.
```

## 旅行纪念珐琅徽章

<a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/0999cbfa1f860086db384602ee5ee5485671cb58/data/images/case543.jpg"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/0999cbfa1f860086db384602ee5ee5485671cb58/data/images/case543.jpg" width="480" alt="Source example"></a>

**Prompt**

```text
Turn the reference photo into a travel souvenir enamel pin badge. Compose it as a SCENE, not a single isolated object. Subject hierarchy: the defining landscape, terrain or landmark of the photo forms the main body of the badge and occupies most of its area. If a person appears prominently in the photo, keep them in the badge as a small, simplified figure at true relative scale within that landscape — the person is an accent, the landscape is the subject. Preserve the original spatial relationship and scale between the figure and the surroundings. How to render the person: flat enamel color blocks matching their real clothing and hair color from the photo. The face is a smooth plain area of light skin-tone enamel with no drawn facial features — do NOT render the person as a dark or black silhouette, and do NOT black out the face or head. Skin reads as a warm light enamel color, clearly lighter than the clothing. Styling: thin polished gold outline around the silhouette and along every internal divider, glossy enamel color fill, gentle even lighting with only a soft sheen on the gold lines, very subtle drop shadow. Outer contour follows the scene's own shape, not a plain rectangle. Background: flat dark navy coarse linen texture. Badge centered, filling about 60% of the frame. Avoid: black silhouette figure, blacked-out face, dark featureless head, portrait close-up, detailed facial features, person dominating the badge, cropping out the landscape, three-quarter angle, macro product photography, heavy specular glare, cartoon, realistic scene, text, watermark.
```

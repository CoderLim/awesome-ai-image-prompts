## Appmockup — habit-tracker iOS app

<a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/appmockup/demo.png"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/appmockup/demo.png" width="480" alt="After"></a>

**Prompt**

```text
Create a photoreal iPhone mockup showing one screen of a habit-tracker app. UI: today's habits, streak count, simple checkboxes, calm typography. Device at a slight angle on a desk, soft daylight. 16:9. Readable UI, not lorem-ipsum blur. No Apple marketing lockup.
```

## Landingpage — indie SaaS waitlist

<a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/landingpage/demo.png"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/landingpage/demo.png" width="480" alt="After"></a>

**Prompt**

```text
Create a desktop screenshot of a marketing landing page for an indie developer tool. Above the fold: short headline, one sentence, email waitlist field, primary button. Below: three benefit tiles. Dark theme, tasteful, not a Dribbble clutter dump. 16:9 crop of the browser content area. No real company trademarks.
```

## Poster — AI conference one-sheet

<a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/poster/demo.png"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/poster/demo.png" width="480" alt="After"></a>

**Prompt**

```text
Create a theatrical conference poster. Title text exactly: "VIBE CODING SUMMIT 2026" Layout: bold title in the top third, one large hero subject (laptop with soft neon rim light), lots of negative space, Swiss / Bauhaus restraint. Colors: near-black background, #7fff6e accent, white secondary type. No tiny unreadable paragraphs. No fake QR codes. No watermarks. 16:9.
```

## Youtubethumbnail — I tested 3 image models

<a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/youtubethumbnail/demo.png"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kondasviktor/vcl-ai-image-prompts/695e086fc3043c0ca39f1ad2b33fdcf3b94a35b4/examples/youtubethumbnail/demo.png" width="480" alt="After"></a>

**Prompt**

```text
Create a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9. Big readable words: I TESTED 3 IMAGE MODELS Three small tiles hinting ChatGPT / Gemini / Grok without official logos if that is cleaner. High contrast, face optional. Not a desktop screenshot. Not a 4K wallpaper. Leave a little safe margin so YouTube crop does not kill the type.
```
